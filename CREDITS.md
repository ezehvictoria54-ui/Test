# Image & font credits

All photography in this demo is royalty-free and sourced from
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), which grants free use for commercial and
personal projects. Images were downloaded and are served locally from
`/public/images/` (never hot-linked) so they load instantly and reliably during
a live demo, even on weak wifi.

> These photos are used here **for demonstration purposes only**. When onboarding
> a real agent, replace them with that agent's own listing photography (see the
> README, "Swapping in a real agent's photos").
